LEGAL / 01

Comprehensive Data Collection & Purpose

Eda operates under a strict data-minimization framework in compliance with Discord Developer Policy 15. We explicitly avoid broad surveillance or bulk logging of casual conversations. We only collect and process data that is strictly necessary for server moderation, security, and utility functions:

  • Message Caching for Audit Logs To reliably provide deleted and edited message tracking for moderators, Eda temporarily caches message content (including text, timestamps, and attachment URLs) in RAM, utilizing a temporary, isolated SQLite database fallback. This localized caching ensures that moderation logs accurately capture original messages before they are permanently deleted by users or AutoMod filters.

    Note: Once an audited event (like a flagged edit or deletion) is securely forwarded to our internal staff log channels by Eda, it remains there as a permanent Discord message for formal moderation record-keeping.
  • Ticket Transcripts When a user utilizes the internal Ticket System to contact staff, the conversation within the isolated ticket thread is compiled into a secure transcript upon closing. This transcript is securely stored for administrative record-keeping, quality assurance, and moderation review. Users are explicitly notified of this transcription process immediately upon opening a ticket.
  • Moderation History & Member Tracking Official infractions (warns, mutes, kicks, bans, and timeouts) are permanently stored in our secure database. This data is strictly linked to your public Discord User ID to maintain an accurate historical moderation record. We also log role states to enforce "sticky roles" (automatically restoring a user's roles if they attempt to bypass a punishment by leaving and rejoining the server).
LEGAL / 02

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar frameworks, we process your data under the legal basis of Legitimate Interest. Our legitimate interest is to provide robust security, enforce server rules, prevent malicious behavior, and maintain a safe community environment for the Dominion Discord server.

LEGAL / 03

Exemptions & Dropped Data

To further minimize our data footprint, certain data is explicitly dropped before processing:

  • Bot Clutter Messages generated by designated automated bots, webhooks, or system integrations are dropped entirely before the caching phase and never enter our localized databases or memory streams.
LEGAL / 04

Strict Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We do not hold your data hostage. We adhere to strict retention schedules based on the utility and security requirements of the server:

  • Caching Purge Schedule Cached message content retained solely for edit/delete tracking is automatically and permanently purged from our temporary SQLite database after a maximum of 14 days.
  • Log Channel Retention Messages actively flagged and forwarded by Eda into internal staff log channels (e.g., edit/delete alert feeds, AutoMod triggers) act as official server security records. These are kept indefinitely within Discord's infrastructure unless manually deleted by a Dominion administrator.
  • Your Right to Deletion You have the right to request the immediate deletion of your temporarily cached message data from our local database. You can trigger this at any time by executing the /request-deletion command within the server.
    • Using this command forwards a formal, interactive request card to our Watchtower administrators.
    • Administrators will review and click an "Approve" button to securely execute the purge of your cached chat logs from our localized storage.
    Critical Exemption
    Official moderation infractions—such as bans, formal warnings, or severe security logs—are retained for critical security purposes under legitimate interest and cannot be purged via this automated command.
LEGAL / 05

Security Measures & No Data Sharing

Your data remains strictly within our ecosystem.

  • Zero Third-Party Commercialization We do not sell, license, share, or disclose your API Data, message content, or metadata to data brokers, advertising networks, or any third-party processors.
  • No AI Training Absolutely no message content, user data, or behavioral metadata is extracted or used to train machine learning models, generative AI, or language models.
  • Infrastructure Security All processed data is stored locally on the Operator's isolated, self-hosted server infrastructure utilizing a secure SQLite database. We do not rely on third-party cloud database providers (like AWS or MongoDB Atlas), significantly reducing the attack surface. Access to the host server is strictly restricted to authorized Dominion operators.

Children's Privacy

Eda operates exclusively on the Discord platform, which explicitly requires all users to be at least 13 years old (or older, depending on local laws). Eda is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the required minimum age.
